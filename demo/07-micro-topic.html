Quick Facts
  • Cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-daro followed a grid-pattern layout with advanced drainage systems, indicating sophisticated urban planning.
  • The Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro is one of the earliest known public water structures, measuring approximately 12m x 7m x 2.4m.
  • Houses were built with standardized baked bricks in a 4:2:1 ratio, and most had private wells and bathrooms connected to covered drains.
